Settings

Predicts planes crossing the moon or the sun from where you stand. Three things, about a minute — your location is what matters most: every ~200 m of error is about a second of timing.

1Where are you shooting from?

Tap the map to put the pin exactly where you stand.

2How high are you?

Your eye is – m above sea level. Roughly 3 m per floor; ten metres either way only matters for low, close planes.

3Sound check

A countdown you can follow with your eye at the viewfinder: a chime when a confident transit is under 30 s away, blips that speed up, then a steady tone while the plane is on the disc. A soft falling chime means it was called off.
Browsers block audio until you press something — the test also unlocks it, so do it before you go out.
